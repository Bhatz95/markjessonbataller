Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through the website of Mark Jesson Bataller may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you inquire about properties, financing options, or related real estate services.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, we may collect information such as your name, email address, mobile number, WhatsApp or Viber number, preferred contact method, current location, preferred Philippine property location, budget or payment preference, and the message you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, identify property options that may match your needs, communicate with you through your selected contact method, explain possible cash, Pag-IBIG, or bank financing pathways, and manage follow-up activities connected to your inquiry.

3. Automated Workflows and Service Providers

This website may send submitted inquiry data to connected workflow tools, including n8n, and to other service providers used to process leads, send notifications, organize inquiries, or support customer communication.

These tools process information only as needed for the workflow configured by Mark Jesson Bataller.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with authorized service providers, property partners, developers, agents, or financing-related contacts when necessary to respond to your request or help you evaluate a property opportunity.

5. Data Retention

Inquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain business records, provide follow-up assistance, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that Mark Jesson Bataller controls, subject to applicable laws and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

You may also ask to stop receiving non-essential follow-up communications.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect submitted information. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Links

This website may link to Facebook, Messenger, developers, property listings, or other third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the website, workflows, service providers, or legal requirements.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact Mark Jesson Bataller using the official contact details published on the website or Facebook page.
